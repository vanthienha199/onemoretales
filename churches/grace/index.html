Welcome

Visiting a church for the first time can feel like walking into someone else's family dinner. We remember that feeling. So we keep it simple: good coffee, honest songs, a message from the Bible you can use on Monday, and people who will learn your name.

What to expect on Sunday

  • Parking

    Free lot behind the building, entrance on Lake Adair Blvd. First-time guests get the reserved spots by the front doors.

  • Kids check-in

    Come 10 minutes early to the Kids desk in the lobby. Secure name tags, and every volunteer is trained and background checked.

  • How long

    About 75 minutes. Songs, a short prayer, a message from the Bible, then coffee and pastries in the courtyard.

  • What to wear

    Whatever you are comfortable in. You will see Sunday dresses and you will see flip-flops. It is Florida.

Questions people ask

Do I need to sign up before I come?

No. Just show up. Filling in the form above only lets us save you a parking spot and meet you at the door.

What happens during the service?

About 25 minutes of songs, a short prayer, and a 35 to 40 minute message from the Bible. You can sing along or just listen. Nobody will single you out.

Is there something for my kids?

Yes, at both Sunday services: nursery for babies, and classes up to 5th grade. You get a matching tag to pick them up, and we text you if they need you.

Do you have a Vietnamese service?

Yes, Sundays at 11:00 AM in the Chapel, led by Pastor Minh Nguyen. Bilingual families often split between the two 11:00 services.

Will I be asked to give money?

No. Guests are our guests. There is a giving box by the courtyard doors and online giving for people who call Grace Harbor home.

Our beliefs

What we believe

  1. One God

    Father, Son and Holy Spirit, who made the world and loves it.

  2. The Bible

    God's word to us: true, trustworthy, and the final word on how we live.

  3. Jesus

    He lived the life we could not, died for our sin, and rose again. We are saved by grace through faith in him.

  4. The church

    Not a building but a family, called to love our neighbors in Orlando and beyond.

  5. Hope

    Jesus will return and make all things new.
