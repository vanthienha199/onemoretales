A word from our pastor

You don't have to know the hymns to belong here.

Some of our members were baptized in this sanctuary in the 1960s. Others walked in for the first time last month. On a Sunday morning you will find both sitting in the same pew, sharing one hymnal.

If you are looking for a church that keeps the old prayers, teaches the Bible plainly, and knows your name by your second visit, we would be glad to meet you. Come early and have coffee with us in the narthex.

Plan a Visit

What to expect on your first Sunday

  • Parking

    Free parking in the main lot off Lakeview Avenue. Spaces nearest the front doors are held for guests, seniors and anyone who needs them. A golf cart runs from the overflow lot on Sundays.

  • Children

    Check in at the Kids' desk in the east hallway and receive a matching tag to pick your child up. All volunteers are background checked. Children are always welcome to stay with you in the pew.

  • How long

    Services run 60 to 75 minutes. Coffee is served in the narthex 30 minutes before every service, and greeters at the front doors can show you to a seat.

  • What to wear

    Whatever you are comfortable in. You will see suits and Sunday dresses at 8:30 and jeans at 10:30. Nobody will mind either way.

  • Accessibility

    Step-free entry at the side door, wheelchair spaces in the front and middle rows, large-print bulletins and hymnals, and a hearing loop in the sanctuary.

Sacraments & Life Events

Baptism, weddings and funerals

Baptism

We baptize infants and adults, usually during the 8:30 or 10:30 service. Families meet once with a pastor beforehand. Adults join a four-week class that begins each season.

Ask about baptism

Weddings

The sanctuary seats 340 and the Fellowship Hall hosts receptions of up to 180. Couples complete premarital counseling with one of our pastors. Members receive priority on the calendar.

Request a wedding date

Holy Communion

Shared on the first Sunday of each month and on holy days. All who trust in Christ are welcome at the Table. Gluten-free bread and grape juice are always offered.

Funerals & Memorials

When you lose someone you love, call the church office any time, day or night. Our pastors will help you plan a service, and the Care Team provides a reception meal.

Our History

1962 to today

Forty-one families met in a school cafeteria on a Sunday in March 1962. This is what has happened since.

  1. 1962

    A congregation in a cafeteria

    St. Andrew's holds its first service in the Lakeview Elementary cafeteria, with a borrowed piano and folding chairs. The church dinners begin the same year.

  2. 1966

    The sanctuary is dedicated

    Members raise the funds to build on four acres of orange grove on Lakeview Avenue. The cornerstone holds a Bible signed by every founding family.

  3. 1974

    The stained glass windows

    Four windows depicting the life of Christ are installed above the altar, given in memory of members lost in the Second World War and Korea.

  4. 1988

    Fellowship Hall opens

    A new hall for Wednesday suppers, youth, and the community food pantry, which now serves over 200 neighbors a month.

  5. 1999

    Vietnamese worship begins

    Twelve Vietnamese families start meeting on Sunday afternoons. Today the 12:30 service is one of our three Sunday congregations.

  6. 2012

    Fifty years, and a new service

    At our 50th anniversary we add the 10:30 contemporary service for younger families, while keeping the 8:30 liturgy unchanged.

  7. Today

    Three congregations, one family

    About 650 people worship with us each week, in two languages, with every service streamed to members at home and around the world.

What We Believe

The faith of the church, plainly said

Each Sunday we say the Apostles' Creed together, as Christians have for centuries.

  1. One God

    Father, Son and Holy Spirit, maker of heaven and earth.

  2. Jesus Christ

    Fully God and fully human, crucified, risen, and coming again.

  3. Holy Scripture

    The Old and New Testaments are the Word of God and our rule of faith and life.

  4. Grace

    We are saved by grace through faith, not by anything we earn.

  5. The Church

    One holy, catholic and apostolic church, gathered around Word and Sacrament.
