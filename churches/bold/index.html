    Plan a visit

    Your first Sunday, sorted.

    Tell us you're coming and someone will meet you at the door, walk your kids to check-in and save you a seat. Nobody will ask you to stand up.

    Parking

    Guest spots are in the front row off Mills Ave. Turn on your hazards and a parking volunteer will wave you in.

    Kids check-in

    Birth to 5th grade. Arrive 10 minutes early, check in at the Kids desk and you get a matching tag for pickup.

    How long

    About 75 minutes. Around 25 minutes of music, a 35 minute message, then coffee on the patio.

    What to wear

    Whatever you'd wear to grab coffee. Shorts and sneakers are normal. This is Florida.

    I'm new

    Takes 20 seconds. We only use this to welcome you.

    Bringing kids?

    See you Sunday, friend.

    We'll email you directions and a parking map.

    About

    What we believe

    The short version. Ask any of us for the long one over coffee.

    1. God

      One God, Father, Son and Holy Spirit, who made everything and is good.

    2. Jesus

      Fully God and fully human. He died for our sin and rose from the dead.

    3. Grace

      Salvation is a gift received by faith in Jesus, not something we earn.

    4. The Bible

      God's inspired word and our final authority for faith and life.

    5. The Spirit

      The Holy Spirit lives in every believer and gives gifts to build the church.

    6. The Church

      Not a building. People who follow Jesus together and serve their city.
